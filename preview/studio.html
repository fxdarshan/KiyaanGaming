A specialist studio for slot games.

Kiyaan Gaming is a slot game studio based in India. We create original slot games, and we offer our team to game studios around the world as a production partner.

What we do

Original games

We design and build our own slot games, from the first idea to a playable prototype. Each game on this site is available for licensing or acquisition.

Production partner

Studios can bring us in to produce a complete game package or to add capacity to their own team. We deliver game art and Spine 2D animation in organised files that are ready for development.

How we work

  1. 01

    Brief

    We learn your theme, audience, market and technical requirements.

  2. 02

    Concept and art

    We develop the look of the game and agree it with you before animation begins.

  3. 03

    Animation

    We bring the approved art to life and review it with you in stages.

  4. 04

    Handoff

    We deliver files your developers can use straight away, and stay available while they integrate them.

Leadership

Kiyaan Gaming is led by senior specialists in slot art direction and animation. Experienced leadership oversees every project, from the first brief to the final delivery.

We focus on slot games only, so the whole team understands how a slot is designed, animated and built.
